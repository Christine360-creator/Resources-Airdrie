Somatic Adrenaline Release

🧱 Wall Push (30s Maximum Force)

Stand an arm's length from a sturdy wall. Place both palms flat and push with 100% of your body strength for 30 seconds. This metabolizes surging adrenaline trapped in muscles.

🧊 Cold Temperature Dive Reflex

Splash ice-cold water onto your cheekbones and eyes, or hold a frozen can against your forehead for 20 seconds. This triggers the mammalian dive reflex to drop pulse rates rapidly.

🦋 Butterfly Tapping

Cross your forearms across your chest so your fingers touch your collarbones. Alternate tapping left and right in a slow rhythm (1 tap per second) while taking slow breaths.

Daily 5-Minute Reset Routine

☀️ Morning (1 Min)

1 double-inhale physiological sigh + write down 1 small concrete task you intend to finish today.

⛅ Midday Pause (2 Min)

4 cycles of Box Breathing + drop shoulder tension and unclench your jaw completely.

🌙 Evening Wind-down (2 Min)

Name 3 micro-comforts from today + 3 slow cycles of 4-7-8 breathing to invite natural rest.
