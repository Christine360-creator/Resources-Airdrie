Physical & Somatic Nerve De-Escalation

When adrenaline is surging, mental tactics alone can fail. Physical counter-signals shift your autonomic nervous system.

🧊 Mammalian Dive Reflex

Splash ice-cold water onto your eyes and cheekbones, or hold a frozen cold pack to your face for 20 seconds. This mechanically lowers heart rate within 30 seconds.

🦋 Butterfly Tapping

Cross your hands over your chest so fingertips rest below your collarbones. Alternate tapping left and right in a slow rhythm (1 tap per second) while breathing slowly.

🧱 Wall Push (Adrenaline Dump)

Place both palms flat against a solid wall. Push with all your physical strength as if trying to move the building for 30 seconds. Discharges trapped adrenaline.

✊ Progressive Clench & Release

Clench both fists, curling your toes, and tightening your shoulders for 7 seconds. Then release entirely, letting your muscles drop loose.
